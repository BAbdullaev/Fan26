Frequently asked questions

How old does my child need to be?+

Following Tennessee guidelines: Kindergartners must turn 5 on or before August 15, 2026. PreK is for children who are 3 or 4 by August 15 (and fully potty-trained).

What documents will I need to enroll?+

The Tennessee basics: a certified birth certificate, an official TN Immunization Certificate, proof of a physical exam within the past 12 months, and proof of residency. Our waitlist form walks you through the full checklist.

What about lunch and snacks?+

Families pack lunch and a water bottle; we provide a morning snack. We're a nut-aware school and will share allergy guidelines before the first day.

Is there before or after care?+

The school day runs 8:00–3:00. We're exploring extended care based on family interest — tell us on the waitlist form if you'd use it!

How much is tuition, and can we pay monthly?+

Tuition is $8,000 per year for both PreK and Kindergarten — the same rate for every grade. Families may pay in full or spread it across 10 monthly payments of $800.

Do students wear uniforms?+

A simple, comfy dress code that lets children play and learn freely. Full details (and where to buy) come with your welcome packet.

How does the waitlist work?+

Submit the form, and we'll confirm within two school days. Seats are offered in order of application, with priority for siblings. No fee is due until a seat is offered.

Ready to save a seat?

Seats are limited for our inaugural year. Join the waitlist today — it only takes five minutes.
