Kindergarten

TN-ELDS aligned

A day at Falah

Our daily rhythm

Predictable enough to feel safe, playful enough to feel magical.

8:00
Arrival & morning circle
Warm hellos, a song or two, calendar time, and the day's big question.
8:30
Literacy time
Phonics games, read-alouds, and small-group reading with the teacher.
10:00
Snack & recess
Fresh air and free play — where some of the best learning happens.
10:45
Math & hands-on centers
Counting bears, pattern blocks, and number games in small groups.
12:00
Lunch together
Packed lunches, table manners, and lots of giggles.
12:45
Rest & discovery
Quiet rest for PreK; science and discovery projects for Kindergarten.
1:45
Art, music & movement
Paint-splattered smocks, rhythm sticks, and dance breaks.
3:00
Storytime send-off
One last story, backpacks on, and home with something to tell you.
