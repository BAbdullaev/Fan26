TN-ELDS aligned

Our curriculum follows the Tennessee Early Learning Developmental Standards — so every playful day still builds exactly the skills Tennessee expects for school readiness. Kindergarten module themes run from the five senses and farms through history and world cultures, and every student takes at least one field trip during the year.

Routines at home help, too

Children thrive when they know what to expect. Consistent routines at home help them feel secure, build independence, and arrive ready to learn. A few simple things that make a real difference:

✦A consistent bedtime and wake-up time
✦Predictable morning and evening routines
✦Advance notice before transitions
✦Visual schedules or pictures of the day
✦Letting children help with simple daily tasks
✦Structure that is predictable, not rigid
