Enroll at Falah Academy

📋

Have these ready

You can start without them, but having them on hand makes it one sitting.

✦Your child’s certified birth certificate
✦Official Tennessee Immunization Certificate
✦Physical exam from the past 12 months
✦Proof of residency — a lease or recent utility bill
✦Emergency contacts and anyone allowed at pickup
✦Your pediatrician’s name, plus any allergies or conditions
💬

Is my child the right age?

Enter a birthday and we’ll tell you which classroom it points to before you start.

Tennessee’s cutoff is August 15. Kindergartners must turn 5 on or before that date; PreK is for children who are 3 or 4 by then and fully potty-trained.

What to expect

Four steps, about twenty minutes

The application walks you through each one in order.

1

Parent details

Who you are and how to reach you — this creates your family account.

2

Your child

Name, birthday, program, and health information. Add more than one child here.

3

Contacts & permissions

Emergency contacts, approved pickups, and the school’s permission forms.

4

Review & submit

Check everything over, acknowledge, and send it to us.

Ready when you are

Questions before you begin? Email admissions@fan2026.org or book a tour — we are glad to walk you through it.
